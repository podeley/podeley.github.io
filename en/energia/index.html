What it solves

Two decisions, before the desk opens

See the system this morning, and where the network binds

A contract gets signed against transport capacity that isn't always where it's assumed to be. Two sheets in one: today's demand, linepack and injection, and the 76 segments of the network month by month with what each one carries. Bolivia went from injecting 12.7 MMm³/d in July 2022 to 0.3 in 2025 and unconventional filled the gap — visible segment by segment. The map's capacity is nameplate, so 8 segments read above 100%: that is declared, not corrected.

ENARGAS and CAMMESA · system daily, network monthly through 2025-09

Price a bid with the risk quantified

The question in an auction is never what the expected marginal cost is, but what being wrong costs in each direction. Merit-order dispatch plus Monte Carlo over hydrology, demand, gas price and renewable resource for Peru's SEIN: what price to bid, and what you lose by overshooting or undershooting. It runs entirely in the browser and the dispatch engine is unit-tested.

How I work

Three sizes of work

The tool gets picked after working out which decision is stuck. Open sources and explicit assumptions, so the desk argues with the number instead of guessing it; an assumption that can't be defended is labeled as one.

Diagnostic

One market question against public data. The result is a memo: the number, the uncertainty around it, and the assumptions holding it up.

Project

The model or the dashboard over your market and your portfolio, with whatever team the case calls for. You get back the tool running wherever you want it.

Advisory

Ongoing input on what to watch each morning and which data is worth buying, if any of it is.

The problem defines the team, not an org chart. What I contribute is the bridge between the commercial decision and the tool; specialists cover the rest — gas and power systems engineering, market modeling, programming, cybersecurity and AI safety. I work with partners, and also with people already on your payroll: whoever runs dispatch every day knows things no model ships with.
